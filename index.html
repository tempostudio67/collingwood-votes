What the mayor does

The mayor chairs council, speaks for the town and sits on Simcoe County council. Staff, led by the chief administrative officer (CAO), run the town day to day. Since 2025 the mayor also holds strong mayor powers, including writing the budget.

Town budget, 2026
$198.4M

$127M for projects like roads and pipes, $71M for yearly operations. The mayor proposes it.

From property taxes
$44.5M

The rest comes from grants, development charges, user fees and reserves.

Votes on council
1 of 9

Mayor, deputy mayor and seven councillors. Strong mayor powers change the math on some votes.

The job
4 years

Full-time since 2022. Base pay was about $62,000 in 2023, plus separate county council pay.

What the deputy mayor does

One of nine votes on council, like any councillor. Usually fills in when the mayor is away. No strong mayor powers.

Votes on council
1 of 9

Same vote as a councillor.

County seat
Gone

Starting this term, only mayors sit on Simcoe County council. Deputy mayors used to sit there too.

The job
4 years

Base pay was about $34,000 in 2023. The extra county council pay ends with the county seat.

Both candidates are sitting councillors. Starting this term, the deputy mayor no longer sits on Simcoe County council.

How to vote

You can vote if you're a Canadian citizen, 18 or older, and you or your spouse own or rent property in Collingwood. Part-time residents qualify.

Online

10 a.m. Thursday, October 8 to 8 p.m. Monday, October 26. Use the credentials in your Voter Information Letter.

In person

Collingwood Public Library, 55 Ste. Marie St. Advance polls October 15 and 22 (4 to 8 p.m.) and October 25 (10 a.m. to 2 p.m.). Election day 10 a.m. to 8 p.m.

Check you're on the list

collingwood.voterservices.ca. No letter by October 8? Call 705-445-1030 ext. 3800.

Catch up on the debates

The all-candidates meetings are done: the environment forum on September 18 and the Chamber of Commerce meeting on September 30. CollingwoodToday has coverage of both, and you can watch the full mayor and deputy mayor debate on Rogers tv.

About this guide

Independent

Not affiliated with any campaign, the Town of Collingwood or any third-party advertiser. It does not endorse anyone.

Same format for everyone

Candidates appear in ballot order. Pen marks point out dates, rules and how things work, never a candidate or a position. Each gets at most three points per issue, and positions are paraphrased from what they've said or published, never inferred.

Record and concept

Record marks something already done in office. Concept marks an idea the candidate calls uncosted.

Gaps

"No stated position found" means it wasn't in the material reviewed, not that the candidate has no position. Check the sources below.
